Teen permit driving hours in California (2026): total, night, what counts

Short answer

  • Total: 50 hours of supervised driving
  • Night: 10 hours of the total

Permy is an independent app, not affiliated with any state agency. Confirm the current requirements with your state agency before relying on them.

What counts as night in California

Night runs from sunset to sunrise. Permy works out sunset and sunrise from where you drive.

Your state's guidance doesn't give exact night hours, so Permy counts night from sunset to sunrise.

Sources

Parent certifies the hours; DMV's log form is optional. For minors: 50 hours of supervised practice including 10 at night. Required professional driver training and other permit conditions are separate.
