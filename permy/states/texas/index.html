Teen permit driving hours in Texas (2026): total, night, what counts

Short answer

  • Total: 30 hours of supervised driving
  • Night: 10 hours of the total

Permy is an independent app, not affiliated with any state agency. Confirm the current requirements with your state agency before relying on them.

What counts as night in Texas

Night starts 30 minutes after sunset and ends 30 minutes before sunrise. Permy works out sunset and sunrise from where you drive.

The state's wording: “Night is 30 minutes after sunset to 30 minutes before sunrise.”

The official guide supplies the +30/-30 night definition. Its older 1-hour daily cap is superseded by current 16 TAC §84.500.

Daily/weekly limit in Texas

Only 2 hours a day of driving counts toward the requirement. On a day you drive more than that, Permy keeps every minute in your log and shows how much of it counts, so your totals match what the state allows.
