What counts as night in Alabama

Night runs from sunset to sunrise. Permy works out sunset and sunrise from where you drive.

Your state's guidance doesn't give exact night hours, so Permy counts night from sunset to sunrise.

Pathways

This state has more than one set of requirements. Which one applies depends on the learner.

  • Supervised-practice pathway: 50 total. The general ALEA GDL page permits 50-hour certification OR qualifying education; a school-specific parental form may require both for its certificate. Confirm the specific education program before selecting the substitution pathway.
  • Qualifying approved driver education: Informational only. The general ALEA GDL page permits 50-hour certification OR qualifying education; a school-specific parental form may require both for its certificate. Confirm the specific education program before selecting the substitution pathway.
