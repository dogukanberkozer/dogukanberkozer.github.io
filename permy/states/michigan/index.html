Teen permit driving hours in Michigan (2026): total, night, what counts

Short answer

  • Total: 50 hours of supervised driving
  • Night: 10 hours of the total
  • Before Segment 2: 30 hours total, 2 hours at night

Permy is an independent app, not affiliated with any state agency. Confirm the current requirements with your state agency before relying on them.

What counts as night in Michigan

Night starts 30 minutes after sunset and ends 30 minutes before sunrise. Permy works out sunset and sunrise from where you drive.

The state's wording: “Nighttime practice is 30 minutes after sunset to 30 minutes before sunrise.”

Sources

A log is shown to the Segment 2 instructor (30 h, 2 night) and to the skills test examiner (50 h, 10 night).
